Frequently Asked Questions

Clear Answers, Zero Guesswork

Everything you need to know about local-first privacy, device transfers, and returns.

Is my data uploaded anywhere?

No. SABOOT is strictly local-first. All shipping label parsing, barcode detection, video recording, and photo collages execute 100% on your workstation in your browser. Videos and images are saved directly to your local hard drive via the Chromium File System Access API (or browser Downloads fallback). Zero customer data, tracking numbers, or video bytes are ever uploaded to any cloud server or database.

What if I change PC or format my workstation?

SABOOT includes soft device binding. You are never locked out. If you upgrade your laptop, switch warehouse packing tables, or format your computer, you can move your active license with 1 click right from the app ("Move license to this device"). Your video files remain safely on your hard drive or external SSD where you saved them.

What is the refund policy?

We offer a Free Forever plan (40 labels/day and 10 recordings/day) so you can test SABOOT thoroughly on your actual warehouse setup before paying anything. For paid licenses, if the software does not work on your setup or hardware, contact support within 7 days of purchase for a prompt refund.

Can I use SABOOT without an active internet connection?

Yes. SABOOT is a true offline-first Progressive Web App (PWA). Once loaded or installed on your desktop, all label vector cropping, barcode scanning, video recording, and license verification run completely offline. You never need an internet connection at the packing table.

What barcode scanners and cameras are compatible?

SABOOT works with any standard USB or Bluetooth handheld barcode scanner gun (HID keyboard emulation mode) and any standard USB webcam or built-in camera. No proprietary hardware or drivers are required.

Are marketplace dispute deadlines guaranteed?

Return dispute filing deadlines (e.g. Meesho SAFE 72h, Flipkart SPF 14 days, Amazon SAFE-T 30 days) are based on published platform guidelines and are marked [needs verification] because marketplaces periodically adjust their seller policies. SABOOT provides real-time configurable countdowns to keep your team ahead of deadlines.
