## Flipkart Seller Protection Fund (SPF) Guidelines

The Flipkart Seller Protection Fund (SPF) exists to compensate sellers for losses incurred due to damaged, defective, or customer-swapped returns, courier transit damage, or fraudulent customer returns.

> **Important Notice:** Flipkart SPF rules, filing eligibility conditions, and dispute time limits cited below require verification [needs verification].

## Essential Filing Rules

### 1. Dispute Filing Deadline [needs verification]
Flipkart SPF guidelines typically require sellers to file dispute tickets within **14 days (336 hours)** of return delivery. It is strongly advised to inspect every return on the day of courier handover.

### 2. Evidence Standards Demanded by SPF Auditors
When reviewing claims, Flipkart compliance teams inspect:
1. **Uncut Inward Video:** Video footage showing the return package being opened for the first time with courier shipping label clearly visible.
2. **Outbound Dispatch Proof:** Verification that the correct SKU and quantity were originally dispatched.
3. **Serial Number & IMEI Tracking:** For electronics, the serial number or IMEI visible on both outbound packaging and return merchandise.

### 3. Step-by-Step Filing Workflow
1. **Unboxing Station Operation:** Scan the return AWB with a barcode scanner gun before cutting the courier seal.
2. **Immediate Damage Flagging:** If an empty box, stone, or swapped garment is found, flag the parcel immediately.
3. **Compile the Dossier:** SABOOT auto-generates side-by-side comparison images between outbound dispatch stills and inward return unboxing stills.
4. **Submit via Seller Hub:** Paste the structured dispute description and attach the high-resolution evidence collage.
