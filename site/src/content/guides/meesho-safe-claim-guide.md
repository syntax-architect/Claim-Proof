## Overview of Meesho SAFE Claims

Meesho sellers facing fraudulent returns, swapped items, or missing merchandise can submit claims under the Supplier Assistance for Fulfillment and Errors (SAFE) policy. To qualify for reimbursement, marketplaces typically require irrefutable, continuous video evidence demonstrating that the item was packaged correctly during outbound dispatch or received defective during return unboxing.

> **Important Notice:** Marketplace dispute policies and filing windows are subject to platform updates. Marketplace rules and filing timelines cited below require verification [needs verification].

## Standard Operating Procedure (SOP)

### 1. The 72-Hour Filing Window [needs verification]
Under prevailing Meesho marketplace guidelines, dispute claims must be submitted within **72 hours** of receiving the return parcel. Missing this cutoff often results in automated claim rejection.

### 2. Required Video Evidence Standards
To prevent claim rejections based on allegations of edited footage:
* **Continuous Uncut Recording:** The video recording must never pause, cut, or skip frames from the initial scan of the Air Waybill (AWB) barcode to the final sealing of the shipping bag.
* **Burnt-in Timestamp Watermark:** The video must display a visible, indelible Indian Standard Time (IST) timestamp showing milliseconds.
* **Clear Barcode Identification:** The courier tracking barcode and Order ID must be clearly readable at the beginning and end of the clip.

### 3. Packaging & Weight Capture
* Show the parcel on the weighing scale before sealing if disputing weight discrepancies.
* Ensure tamper-evident security tape or security flyers are visibly identifiable in the footage.

### 4. Assembling the Claim Dossier
When submitting your dispute on the Meesho Supplier Panel:
1. Select the precise dispute reason (e.g., *Wrong Item Returned*, *Empty Box*, or *Damaged Merchandise*).
2. Attach the side-by-side photo collage capturing the 4 critical moments (AWB scan, product placement, sealing, and final package).
3. Provide the local link or upload the uncut MP4 video file recorded at your SABOOT packing station.
